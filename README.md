# ♠ Shengji Academy

**Learn 升级 (Shengji) — the Chinese trick-taking card game — in nine short lessons.**

Shengji (升级, literally "level up," also known as **Tuolaji** / 拖拉机, "Tractor") is one of the most popular card games in China, but it has a reputation for being hard to pick up from a rulebook alone. Shengji Academy breaks it down into short, bilingual lessons — each capped with a quick quiz — so you can go from "never played" to "ready to sit at the table" in about half an hour.

[![Try it now](https://img.shields.io/badge/Try%20it%20now%20%E2%86%92-1C8F68?style=for-the-badge)](https://y2749zha.github.io/level-up/)

## What's inside

Nine lessons, each with a short reading and a multiple-choice quiz:

1. **Welcome to Shengji** — the four players, two teams, one shared rank
2. **Counting Points** — which cards are worth what
3. **The Ladder** — how a team's rank climbs from 2 to Ace
4. **The Main Order** — how trump cards outrank everything else
5. **Calling the Main** — how a team decides what's trump
6. **The Deal & the Bottom Cards** — dealing and the hidden "kitty"
7. **Playing a Trick** — legal plays, combinations, and following suit
8. **Keeping Score** — how a hand is won or lost
9. **Sit at the Table** *(capstone)* — a full review before your first real game

## Features

- **Bilingual** — every lesson and quiz is written in both English and 简体中文, with a one-click toggle
- **Learn at your own pace** — each lesson unlocks the next; the capstone review unlocks once you've finished the rest
- **Quizzes with instant feedback** — see why an answer is right or wrong before moving on, and earn stars for how well you did
- **Your progress is yours** — everything is saved locally in your browser. No account, no sign-up, nothing sent to a server
- **Works everywhere** — a lightweight page that loads fast on desktop or mobile, with a light/dark theme that follows your system settings

## Who it's for

Anyone who's been invited to a Shengji game and wants to show up knowing the rules — or anyone curious about one of China's most-played card games.
